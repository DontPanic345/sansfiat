Paint Army

Turn 1. Select a captain (★), Paint tiles to add them to his zone (an unbroken chain from his own tile, diagonals ok); Unpaint removes them. At end of turn the whole army spreads evenly over the zone, one tile per turn. Painting deploys a captain; Undeploy (D) clears the zone, and the army then simply follows him wherever he marches. Merge folds troops on his tile into him. His troops split evenly across the chain and creep out one tile per turn. A tile is only yours while a unit stands on it (or it is near your city). Idle troops entrench (up to +75% defence). Click an adjacent enemy to charge. Capture the enemy city to win.
