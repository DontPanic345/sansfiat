Paint-the-Territory Armies

Question: Does "paint tiles, and the captain's army divides itself across them" let you capture and hold ground cheaply without feeling broken? Specifically: does the equal split, the light per-tile combat, entrenchment for defenders, and the chance for a lone defender to slip away from a bunched-up charge produce interesting trade-offs? Open the guided walkthroughs below, or poke at it freely.

Map

Click a captain (or army in the list) to select it. Paint territory, click tiles, then Send army. Orange = tiles you're painting now. Coloured frame = tiles an army is holding. →n = troops assigned there. Pips under a tile = entrenchment.
