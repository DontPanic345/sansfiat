How to play Phases. In the pre-game clans forage, trade and settle with no player; the markets that tame the most grain become civs. At founding you pick one to lead (or watch all-AI). The main game then runs turn by turn until one civ holds every founding city (conquest) or the turn limit picks the most populous civ (prosperity).
You control (side panel, your civ only):
  • Projects: what each city builds (granary, storehouse, wall...).
  • Gifts: food to unaligned clans so they lean toward you and join.
  • Scouts: click your scout, then a tile to send it; it reveals land and trail costs.
  • Armies: select a captain, paint its zone; detachments spread, fight and capture.
  • Porters and supply: keep armies fed from cities and caches.
Keys: Space step / end turn · P play · G fast-forward · K skip pre-game · Esc clear selection · D depletion · 1–0, -, = map views. Click a tile to inspect it.
