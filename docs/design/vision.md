# Vision

Sans Fiat (working title) means "without fiat money". It's a game that doesn't use the
typical abstractions of the 4X genre.

1. There is no money or gold that the player has in their coffers, that lets the player buy
   things or interact with the game.
2. Any resource gains its value because the citizens (actors) in the game desire it. If
   something does the job of money (shells, beads, markings on clay balls and so on), that
   item is produced in the game by an actor and traded. Its value comes from what the other
   actors will pay for it.

The era is the first civilisations: the spread of agriculture and animal domestication.
