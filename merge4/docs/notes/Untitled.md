The game is called Sans Fiat (working title) - Without Fiat money. I want a game that doesn't use the typical abstractions of the 4x genere. 

1. There is no money or gold that player 'has in their coffers' that allows the player to buy things or interact with the game.
2. Any resource gains its value because the citizens (actors) in the game desire it. If there was to be somehting doing the job of money, shells, beads, markings in clay balls etc. then that item is produced in game by an actor and traded. its value comes from what the other actors will pay for it.

The era I'm targeting is the first civiations, the spread of agriculture and animal domestication.

---

The pre-game. 

Early humans are nomadic hunter gatherers, they have had enough time to spread accros the available lands, but their total population is low.

starting from a few random seed points. humans spread out hunting and gathering from the land, when it gets depleated, they move on. Each turn, if they are fed, their number increase. when a single clan is large enough, it divides.

For the purposes of the game, lets say a clan can harvest from its tile and nearest neghbours.

A particularly bountiful spot may have inhabitance for almost the full time, perhaps where a river meets a cloast. These sites are were our game begins

---

The double coincidence of wants

There are many different food resources. Clans will have a surplus of some but not another, so they trade.

The game is a complex economy simulation. Actors trade at markets, (rather than in pairs which would be far to complicated). At the market traders post their supply and their demains. The algorithem behind the market then decides how distribute the resources fairly. 

A few of the best performing sites, not too close to eachother become the first markets. Clans can trade if a market is accessable.

---

Game start.

At some point, when the pre-game has settled down the accessble lands have been colonised. The game starts

The markets also become the first civs. One gets assigned to the player and the NPCs.

The clans remain unalligned and peaceful.
