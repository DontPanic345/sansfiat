# Spec-review step

Read the spec, the briefs and `steps.json` the way the sessions after you will: each brief
with only the code beside it. Fix whatever would trip them up, such as a brief missing what
its build needs, a test whose numbers can't pass on the current map, work the piece didn't
ask for, or a model or effort whose `why` doesn't hold. Say what you cut from the piece in
your result's `"scopeCut"`.
