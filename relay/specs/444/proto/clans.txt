## What "moving" is in the live model

A clan's `since` counts turns since it last moved. `clans::move_on` sets it to 0 and the `eat` step adds 1 later in
the same turn, so a clan that moved on reads `since` 1 in the view that turn. A daughter sent off is born with `since`
0 after `eat` (moves.jsonl: born rows at since 0), and reads 1 the turn after. A half from a split keeps its
mother's `since`. A home that drifts a tile to follow its territory's middle keeps `since` too: moves.jsonl shows
most home changes at since 100 to 400, which are drifts, not a clan walking anywhere. So `since ≤ 1` is "arrived at
this home this turn (or, for a daughter, last turn)": 0 to 10 clans per map per turn.
