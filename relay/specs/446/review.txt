The cost is the drawImage call, about 2.7 us each whatever the piece's size. At k 6 to 12 on medium 9, 76 to 95% of the
pieces are on screen, so culling by tile, caching positions or ImageBitmaps save at most the 0.3-0.8 ms of the loop
and 0.5 ms of the bitmaps, where medium9-units-edge needs about 5 ms cut and medium9-places-edge about 8. A full redraw
can't reach the bar with the same draw calls. What can: fewer redraws. A pan moves the camera by whole css px
(panBy's mouse deltas), so a static layer shifted by whole device px draws the same pixels as main's full redraw at the
new camera (round(a + n) = round(a) + n), and only the strips the pan uncovers need drawing.
