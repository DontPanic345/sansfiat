# Rates

What a step costs, for the spec's estimates. The retro replaces this file after each
milestone.

## Measured in M1

All 14 steps ran at high effort. List $ per session:

| Model | Spec | Spec-review | Build | Review | 5-hour points per $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sonnet high | 0.93 | 0.84–0.90 | 0.79–1.96 | 1.51–1.65 | about 7 |
| opus high | 3.38 | | 3.53–4.74 | | about 5 |

Both took about 0.7 weekly points per $. A build that wrote the page or ran sweeps sat at the
top of its range.

## Guesses

Nothing else has run, so these are guesses; an estimate that uses one says so.

- Medium effort costs about 0.7 of high, and low about 0.5, on the same model.
- Haiku costs about a third of Sonnet at the same effort.
- So Opus low comes to about half of Opus high, or 1.5 times Sonnet high.
